History & Journey

The milestones that define our path to excellence.

2010

The Founding

Established with a vision to redefine rehabilitative care, starting with a single clinic and a dedicated team of three specialists.

2015

Expansion

Opened three new specialized centers focusing on sports injuries and post-operative recovery.

2019

Integration of Tech

Introduced advanced biometric screening and digital progress tracking into standard patient care protocols.

2023 - Present

Robotic Care Era

Pioneered the use of advanced robotic assistance devices in complex neuro-rehabilitation programs.
